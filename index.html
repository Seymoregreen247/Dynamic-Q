Write an NFC Tag

Put a website, menu, payment page, or contact-page link on a physical NFC tag. This web app writes tags; it does not turn your phone into a tag.

Leave off for blank tags. Switch on only when you intend to replace a tag you own. Writing leaves the tag rewritable. Erase Tag clears existing NDEF contents with an empty record; it does not unlock locked tags or securely wipe all memory.

1. Choose a link. 2. Tap Write Tag. 3. Hold a writable NDEF tag against the back of your phone.

Use Chrome on an NFC-equipped Android phone, NFC enabled, and the live HTTPS site. Keep this page open and the phone unlocked. After writing, test the tag with another phone.
